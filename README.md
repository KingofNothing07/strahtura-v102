# Strahtura

### Two empires. One board. The valley stays standing.

**[Play Strahtura](https://kingofnothing07.github.io/strahtura-v102/)**

For ninety years Fulgar and Angtapa bled the same valley. Lightning from the white cliffs. Fire from the black hills. When neither king could feed another spring, they met in a ruined chapel and set the war down on a board.

A helm turned one way is a knight. Turned over, it is a noble. Two knights ride as cavalry. Two nobles become a king. A lord may leave court, raise a tower, and step out as a wizard. An archmage can turn that tower into a thing that walks through your own line.

You have two commands each turn. Spend them to advance, stack, trade a title, recruit a fallen knight in full view of the enemy, or break the seal on the one relic you brought to the table. There is only one of each relic. Black chooses first. White strikes first.

Win by taking every enemy noble. A king counts as two.
