# Strahtura

### Two empires. One board.

Pronounced **strah-TOO-rah**.

# [Play Strahtura v1.02](https://kingofnothing07.github.io/strahtura-v102/)

This repository is **only** that version. Open the link. It plays in the browser, on a phone or a computer. No install.

On a phone, open the play link and choose **Add to Home Screen**. The icon is the bolt and the flame. After the first load it plays with the network off.

For ninety years Fulgar and Angtapa bled the same valley. Lightning from the white cliffs. Fire from the black hills. When neither king could feed another spring, they met in a ruined chapel, turned a knight's helm over until it became a crown, and set the war down on eight squares by eight.

You start with fourteen knights and two nobles. A helm one way is a knight. Turned over, it is a noble. Two knights ride as cavalry. Two nobles become a king, and a king may spend both actions, even changing direction. A lord may leave court, raise a tower, and step out as a wizard. Two wizards make an archmage. An archmage may flip into an **Archmage's Tower** (double tower in the board game): a larger gate. Your own pieces pass through it, and it pushes back the way they came.

You have two commands each turn. The first turn of the game has one. A knight steps forward, captures on the diagonal, retreats one square, or uses Front Line to jump the knight directly ahead. Spend the other command to stack, trade a title, recruit a fallen knight in full view of the enemy, or break the seal on the one relic you brought.

There is only one of each relic. Black chooses first. White strikes first. A relic is used once, then it goes back in the box.

You win by taking every enemy noble. A king counts as two. If each side has nothing left but a single noble, the chapel calls it a draw.

## This playtest

- **Knight.** One square forward. Two forward on its first move from your first or second row. One square straight back, without capturing, onto an empty square, your own knight, or your own tower or archmage. Front Line jumps the single knight directly ahead.
- **Archmage's Tower.** An archmage flips into it. It swaps with a friendly piece beside it. It cannot be promoted. It can be unstacked into two Wizard Towers. A piece may enter and leave, and may only step out onto an open square touching the tower.
- **Charge** replaces Shield Wall. A knight, a cavalry, or a noble moves farther than it normally can, up to twice as far in one straight direction, and may capture once if that is already how it captures. It costs both actions. A king, a wizard, an archmage, and either tower cannot charge.
- **Flank** is one action, and only once in the game. One cavalry steps a single square left or right and may capture there.
- **Swordsmanship** is not a shot. The noble, or the king, steps onto the square and takes the piece that was standing there.
- **Sapper's Sacrifice** spends a knight or a cavalry to kill one enemy piece touching it, including a Wizard Tower or an Archmage's Tower.
- **Stone Golem** moves one Wizard Tower a single square, and that tower can destroy an enemy tower or an Archmage's Tower.
- **Time.** Untimed, or a 5, 10, 20, or 30 minute game. The clock is split evenly, and it runs only on your own turn. A 5 minute game is two and a half minutes each.
- **Points settle a flag and a long tournament.** Knight 1, cavalry 3, noble 5, king 10, wizard 7, Wizard Tower 7, archmage 9, Archmage's Tower 9. Your score is every piece still on the board plus every piece you have captured. The higher total wins. A tie is a draw.

Easy spends its relic early. Medium looks for a clear capture. Hard holds the relic until the moment is worth it, and if it picks second it leans toward an answer.

Settings opened during a match close with the ✕ at the top right.
