# Strahtura

### Two empires. One board.

Pronounced **strah-TOO-rah**.

# [Play Strahtura](https://kingofnothing07.github.io/strahtura-v102/)

Open the link. It plays in the browser, on a phone or a computer.

On a phone, open the play link and choose **Add to Home Screen**. The icon is the bolt and the flame. After the first load it plays with the network off.

**Download** at the top of the menu saves **Play Strahtura.html**. That one file has the pieces, the crests, and the sound inside it. Open it. It does not need a folder beside it.

For ninety years Fulgar and Angtapa bled the same valley. Lightning from the white cliffs. Fire from the black hills. When neither king could feed another spring, they met in a ruined chapel, turned a knight's helm over until it became a crown, and set the war down on eight squares by eight.

You start with fourteen knights and two nobles. A helm one way is a knight. Turned over, it is a noble. Two knights ride as cavalry. Two nobles become a king, and a king may spend both actions, even changing direction. A knight steps forward, captures on the diagonal, retreats one square, or uses Front Line to jump the knight directly ahead. A lord may leave court, raise a tower, and step out as a wizard. Two wizards make an archmage. An archmage may flip into an **Archmage's Tower**, the double tower: a larger gate with a portal door. Your own pieces pass through it and step out on one open square beside it, and the tower pushes back the way they came.

You have two commands each turn. The first turn of the game has one, and it is not spent stacking or opening a relic. The other command may stack, trade a title, recruit one fallen knight in full view of the enemy, or break the seal on the relic you brought.

There is only one of each relic. Black chooses first. White strikes first. A relic is used once, then it goes back in the box.

**Charge** sends a knight, a cavalry, or a noble up to twice as far in one straight direction. It spends both actions and captures one piece, if that piece already captures that way.

**Flank** is one action, once in the game. A cavalry steps one square left or right and may capture there.

**Swordsmanship** sends a noble, or a king, one square diagonally forward onto an enemy. The piece takes that square.

**Sapper's Sacrifice** spends one knight beside an enemy piece, and both are removed. A Wizard Tower or an Archmage's Tower falls the same way. That knight returns to your graveyard and may be recruited on a later turn. One knight may be recruited each turn. Sellswords is the relic that brings more.

**Stone Golem** steps one Wizard Tower a single square. That step can take an enemy piece, or destroy an enemy tower.

You win by taking every enemy noble. A king counts as two. If each side has nothing left but a single noble, the chapel calls it a draw.

A game may be untimed, or 5, 10, 20, or 30 minutes, split evenly. The clock runs only on your own turn. A tournament may also stop after 100 turns. When a clock or that limit runs out, points decide: the pieces still on the board, plus every piece you have captured. Knight 1, cavalry 3, noble 5, king 10, wizard 7, Wizard Tower 7, archmage 9, Archmage's Tower 9. The higher total wins. Equal points is a draw.

Easy spends its relic early. Medium looks for a clear capture. Hard holds the relic until the moment is worth it.
